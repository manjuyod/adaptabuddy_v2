What Changed

Logo: no longer a compact title above the form. It becomes a wide, low-opacity cinematic opening-title layer across the upper middle of the scene.

Fields: email and password stay centered beneath the title, with enough contrast and glow to read over the village.

Buttons: actions stack vertically below the fields, closer to a JRPG title menu. The active/default action gets a subtle side-marker/focus treatment rather than a large button panel.

Assets: environment layers still do the scene-setting: asymmetrical houses, natural greenery, foreground path, lanterns, light haze, and save-point glow.
