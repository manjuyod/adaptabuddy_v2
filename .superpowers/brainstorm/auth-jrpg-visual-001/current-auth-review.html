What the current direction is doing well

The background already gives us a strong HD-2D village mood. The title is elegant, and the form placement keeps the art visible instead of covering it with a large panel.

What is not reading as RPG yet

The controls float without a game-world anchor. The email/password fields and actions are very small against a busy scene, especially on mobile. There is no clear menu surface, cursor, save point, gate sign, lantern, or prop cluster that says “this is where the player starts.”

A

Environment-first pass

Keep the current airy/cinematic form. Generate town-gate props around it: lamps, signpost, trees, small houses, foreground cobblestone, subtle save glow.

B

Menu-first pass

Add a readable JRPG menu panel for the form first, then use generated assets as supporting atmosphere.

C

Hybrid light menu

Use a thin, translucent game-menu plate and add village props, but avoid the heavy pixel panel from the earlier sketch.
